# Settle

> A better way for hospitality to get paid.

## Summary

**Settle** is an AI agent that helps hospitality operators monitor, forecast, reconcile, and recover outstanding food and beverage (F&B) balances across multiple locations.

Settle helps hospitality businesses reduce outstanding balances, automate reconciliation, and collect payments more efficiently.

## Positioning

We're **not** pitching a solution to members skipping out on their bills.
We **are** pitching a better way for hospitality to get paid.

## What the agent does

| Capability | Description |
|---|---|
| **Monitor** | Tracks outstanding F&B balances across every location in one view |
| **Forecast** | Projects which balances are likely to come in late or stay unpaid |
| **Reconcile** | Automatically matches payments to balances and accounts |
| **Recover** | Collects outstanding balances efficiently |

## Target users

- Hospitality operators running multiple locations
- Venues that carry F&B balances: clubs, hotels, resorts, and member-based venues

## Outcomes

- Lower outstanding balances
- Automated reconciliation
- Faster, more efficient payment collection
