# Accessibility on Mastercard Developers
source: https://developer.mastercard.com/platform/documentation/accessibility/index.md

Every day, developers around the world leverage Mastercard Developers to deliver world-class solutions to consumers, banks, small business owners and more. We're committed to delivering a platform that is accessible to all of our users. We understand that accessibility is not just about compliance, but about connecting with all of our users, including those with disabilities. That's why we're constantly learning, testing, and adapting our site to improve its accessibility and usability.

## Adherence to standards {#adherence-to-standards}

We have chosen the [Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG/), Level AA, as our accessibility target. Following these guidelines helps us provide a site that's accessible to all people, including those with disabilities. These guidelines not only help us make our site more accessible, but they also make our site more user-friendly for all of our users.

As technology advances, we ensure our content remains robust enough to be interpreted reliably by a wide variety of user agents, including assistive technologies. As part of this commitment, we regularly conduct testing and accessibility audits to ensure our adherence to the WCAG 2.2 standards.

## Join us in our journey {#join-us-in-our-journey}

We invite you to join us in our journey towards creating a more inclusive digital world.

Mastercard Developers is a platform with frequent feature releases and daily content changes across all of our technical documentation. This means we may have site content and capabilities that are still in the process of becoming accessible. If you encounter any accessibility barriers or have suggestions for improvement, [please let us know](https://developer.mastercard.com/support/). Your feedback is a valuable part of helping us make our platform more accessible.

We believe our users are our best resource for understanding how we can improve. So whether you're a developer, a product manager, or someone who's just passionate about accessibility, we want to hear from you.
