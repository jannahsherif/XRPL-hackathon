# Edit Service Details
source: https://developer.mastercard.com/platform/documentation/managing-your-project/editing-service-details/index.md

Service details are specific to the services within your project. Available fields vary by service and may include details such as customer identifiers, customer's contact email and other information.

## Steps for Editing Service Details {#steps-for-editing-service-details}

1. Log in to [Mastercard Developers](https://developer.mastercard.com/account/log-in/). ![log-in](https://static.developer.mastercard.com/content/platform/img/login.png)
2. Select your project from **My projects** page. ![projects](https://static.developer.mastercard.com/content/platform/img/projects.png)
3. On the **Summary** page, under **Project APIs** , find the service you want to edit. ![summary-page](https://static.developer.mastercard.com/content/platform/img/summary.png)
4. Click More options **(⋮)** next to the service.
5. Select **View details** . ![view-details](https://static.developer.mastercard.com/content/platform/img/view-details.png)
6. On the details page, fill in or modify the editable fields as needed. Some fields may not be editable and will appear disabled. The specific fields you can edit will vary depending on the service. ![service-details](https://static.developer.mastercard.com/content/platform/img/service-details.png)
7. Once you've made the necessary edits, click **Update** to save your changes or click **Cancel** to exit without saving.
